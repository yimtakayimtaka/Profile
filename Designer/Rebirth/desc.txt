Created in 3D Studio Max, Rebirth features textures which are drawn by hand and finshed off in photoshop to give it a real arty look.

Haq is the agent who is given the mission to infiltrate the base and battle Ganda213bc2.  Using his trusty bike which can also transform into a robot he is prepared for just about anything.

The music in the background is called "Night of Fire" and is from the anime Initial D.

Voices for Haq and for Ganda213bc2 were recorded myself.

The textures for haq are also created by myslef and are of myself.

This animation took me 4 days to render at 640x480 resolution MJPEG AVI.

This animation was created for a module in univeristy.
