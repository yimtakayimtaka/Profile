The brief was to choose a scenario from the Worst Case Scenario book and present it in an informative electronic format.  We had full freedom in the way and style we chose to present it in.

I chose to present it in the style of a game as i find games are the easiest and most fun way to learn.

You follow the adventure of Kit,Jig and Grandpa.  Kit learns of a mysterious girl from his french class who wishes to meet him all alone the jungle, not being able to resist he sets off.  
  When Jig finds out he needs an excuse to acompany Kit as he can't let Kit have all the fun so he says he will help Kit on his journey.  No sooner as they both decide to leave does Grandpa find out and decides to join them aswell.  
  Fearing Grandpa wil ruin their style they both sneak off while Grandpa is getting changed.  In the jungle Kit sees bubbles in the water and investigates only to find it's an aligator!!.  He asks Jig for help but he doesn't want to ruin his hair!!.  
  Out of nowhere comes Grandpa and instructs Kit step by step how to escape from an aligator.  Kit thanks Grandpa for saving his life.  The End.

The full presentation was created in Flash.  The actors were recorded and rotoscoped.  The voices for Kit and Jig were their own while the voices for all other characters was recorded by myself.

3D scenes were created in Discreet Plasma and exported as swf and imported into Flash.
