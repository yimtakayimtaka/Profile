The brief was to create a short 90 second animation in 3D Studio Max.  We were to model and texture the HMS Victory acurately and animate it accordingly.

The animation shows flowing water, multiple explosions, destructive scenery, flapping flags, bi-pedal characters and much more.

The project used lathing and lofting techniques and a bit of character studio aswell.

This animation was created for a module in univeristy.
