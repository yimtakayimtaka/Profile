Developer Sub-Project

Dark Shadow

Aim

After you fell asleep after work, you wake up to find everyone has left. You are locked in and must find your way out.

Look out for

Many graphics are not present so some flashing blobs might be enemies whilst some might be doors and some might even be people who want to give you a lift.

It’s using

The map editor and script/enemy/item/magic/weapon/armour editor was made in Visual Basic. The character and enemy animation editor was made in flash. The files were linked together e.g. The enemy file “soldier” loaded up the “soldierhard” skeleton in flash and then loads all the properties such as health, moves, magic, speed from the text file created in Visual Basic.

  • Able to have complicated and intricate dialogue with NPC
  • Dynamic isometric engine
  • Ability to destroy and move tiles on the map
  • Dynamic loading and unloading of maps
  • Dynamic battle animations that re-use old animations for same enemies.
  • Battles take into account defence, speed, luck ,strength, magical attributes and much more.
  • All files are externally loaded so can be updated and changed easily.
  • Over 25 uniquely animated enemies each with its own move set.
  • Custom made music.

History

I have been working on this game for over a year now. It’s taken a long time because I have been creating the development tools to edit the map and the animations for battle. I have also been designing and creating the enemies and levels. I have also tried to create and record my own music for the game, it has been hard as I can’t play the keyboard!!.

Controls

Use the keys to move around.

Z = to climb, to talk to NPC, to accept a dialogue/menu option

A = to cancel out of the current menu

S = to open up the menu to customise/edit character properties.

Rules

Explore. To enter the room. Look for the flashing blob infront of the doorways. Move the characters feet ontop of the blobs and press Z. Accept to enter the room to enter it.

Quick Note

I am trying to implement moving/animated platforms which the hero can interact with and as such all the graphics have been removed from the game for me to debug easily. If you want to see a better demonstration you will have to give me an interview!!!.
