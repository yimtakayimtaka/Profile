Developer Sub-Project

BZB

Aim

To aid the bee in getting to the end of the level whilst trying to collect as many flowers as you can along the way.

Look out for

If the bee does not seem to respond properly then remember to alter the recording level of the microphone in flash settings.[right hand click the flash movie then click settings then click on the microphone]. Also enable reduce echo if you can.

It’s using

This and the monkey game use the same engine to load and render the map. It reads the mic input and calculates the level of volume. 100% volume represents the full height of the current level and 0% volume represents the floor of the level.

Levels are loaded externally and the maps are created dynamically.

Map data was created with a custom made map editor in Visual Basic. They are compressed and saved using a custom RLE algorithm and in flash the levels are decompressed again and used.

History

An original project which inspired the monkey game.

Planning on adding more insects to play as such as ladybird(medium speed) and butterfly(slow speed).

Controls

Click Start button to begin

Click “allow” to enable the mic input to the game

Click the long black button with a red outline to load and start the level.

Make a BUZZ sound into the mic. The louder the sound the higher the bee will fly. Once the bee is off the ground it will automatically fly forward at a set pace.

Rules

Yellow flowers save the game. Once you die you will resume from the last yellow flower you landed on.

Red flowers are exit points and end the current level.

Blue flowers are pickups and you are awarded one point for each blue flower you collect.

If you touch any other tile other than the above you lose and are sent to the last save point you landed on.
