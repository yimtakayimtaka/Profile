Deal or No Deal

Aim

To win more money than is in your box or make the best deal and win against the banker.

Look out for

You may have to restart the game once you have played all the way through once.

It’s using

Real movieclips skewed and rotated to act as polygons. Many of these are used to render the boxes you see on screen. The lids of the boxes are opened by manipulating the transform matrix in the point rotation algorithm.

History

I first used this to render a 3d vase containing flowers that rotated around and have since been waiting to use it in a game.

It’s based on the hit TV game show starring Noel Edmonds.

Controls

Use left/right keys to move through the boxes

At start press DOWN key to choose your box

Press UP to open the selected box

When presented with the option click “deal” or “no deal”

Rules

Open the requested number of boxes each round

Aim to get rid of all the blue boxes first as this will raise the bankers offer.

If you dealt at a value higher than the value of your chosen box or you dealt at the dealer’s highest offer or you chose to open your chosen box and it contained more than the bankers highest offer. If you met any of the above conditions then you win.
