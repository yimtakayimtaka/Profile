Designer Sub-Project

Original Game Music

The music was created by imagining the music in my head then I wrote notes on the keyboard and tried to play the music until it sounded like what I wanted, I then wrote down the notes to each section. I then later used those notes to compose the music in an audio application and layer the final music.
